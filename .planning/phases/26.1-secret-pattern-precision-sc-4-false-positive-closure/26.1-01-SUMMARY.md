- **Left `tools/hooks/secret_scan.py` untouched** — its hardcoded `PATTERNS` list (module-level, not reading `gate-registry.json`) is a documented divergence seam per the plan's threat model (T-26.1-05, resolved as not-a-risk for this plan's scope). - **Ratification recorded as a genuine human "approved" reply at the plan's blocking checkpoint** — distinct from and not conflated with any `HARNESS_DEV_BYPASS` dev-session setting used to permit the write itself.

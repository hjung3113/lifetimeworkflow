### Policy correction recorded for ratification

The earlier Phase 6 wording said STRICT and CONTROLLED both required rollback evidence. The ratified runtime policy (`harness/risk-policy.toml`) and transition contract make rollback a CONTROLLED-only obligation; the fixture table and how-to follow that authority. If humans want rollback for STRICT, that is a separate breaking policy and transition-contract change requiring a new ratification and ADR approval; this phase does not make that change.

Promotion coverage is deliberately part of this draft table: T-01/T-02/T-03/T-04 cover `unclear_rollback`, `external_contract_break`, `constitution_plane_touch`, and `repeated_constraint_violation`; C-01 is a score-15 CONTROLLED task with the lower `auth_authorization` promotion flag, proving promotion never lowers an already higher score lane.

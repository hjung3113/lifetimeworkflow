<objective>
Implement the ledger and DOCSUP-03's named anti-rubber-stamp mechanism: disposition/digest coherence
against the previous COMMITTED ledger.

Purpose: this is the control the whole phase exists to provide. The attack is concrete — an agent or
a hurried human sees `STALE_REQUIRED`, pastes the live `source_digest` into the ledger, writes
`disposition = "updated"`, and the gate turns green with the target document untouched. Digests
ALONE cannot detect it: after the paste they are consistent by construction. Only a comparison
against the previous committed ledger can. That is why the paste-the-live-digest row is this plan's
adversarial centrepiece and must be shown RED first.

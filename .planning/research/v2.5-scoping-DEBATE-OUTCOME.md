   remaining CI, or not at all. §0 states this trade explicitly.
2. **No doc-staleness detector.** `docs_sync` + `memory_regen` keep the machine-owned pages true;
   human prose correctness rests on the person reading the PR.
3. **The core no longer promises golden parity** — each instance owns that evidence.
4. **A skill `description` rewrite silently changes routing** once `registry.lock` is gone. Visible
   in the diff; nothing else catches it.
5. **GEN-04 stays hardcoded**, including its `설비`/`wafer` prose tier, until a real forbidden edge
   appears.
6. **The bash surface stays uncovered by design**, recorded in ADR-0012 as a permanent residual so it
   does not return as debt next milestone.

   `orchestrator` plus GSD.

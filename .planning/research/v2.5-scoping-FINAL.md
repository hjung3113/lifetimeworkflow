## 7. Residual risk, accepted knowingly

1. **No in-session guard of any kind** — no constitution, secret, commit, ledger or resume deny, and no
   replacement secret CI job. A bad change reaches a branch and is caught by review or the remaining
   CI, or not at all. §0 states this trade explicitly.
2. **No doc-staleness detector.** `docs_sync` + `memory_regen` keep machine-owned pages true; human
   prose correctness rests on the person reading the PR.
3. **The core no longer promises golden parity** — each instance owns that evidence.
4. **A skill `description` rewrite silently changes routing** once `registry.lock` is gone. Visible in
   the diff; nothing else catches it.
5. **GEN-04 stays hardcoded**, including its `설비`/`wafer` prose tier, until a real forbidden edge
   appears.
6. **The bash surface stays uncovered by design**, recorded in ADR-0012 as a permanent residual.
7. **The product's lifecycle is one prose procedure** in the emitted `orchestrator` persona (route
   selection, 4 routes, six-field completion contract, resume via `.memory/state/activeContext.md`)
   plus `/flow`. No router agent, no state machine, no gate. Accepted: a ~250-line persona carries the
   whole procedure and **nothing detects a weak model skipping a step** — the completion block is
   observable in its output and in the PR, and that is the only check.
8. `.workflow/tasks/` on-disk data is deleted; git has it.
9. **Phase 42's install-set fix is unproven until a real target repo exists** — it corrects a
   demonstrable gap (`destinations.py:142-181` has no `tools/**`), but end-to-end product installation
   is only really exercised by phase 50(b).

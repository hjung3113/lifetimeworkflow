## Milestone

**v2.4 — Enforcement Integrity, Carried Debt, Lane Discipline** (phases 30–37)

**Goal:** close the gap between what this harness *declares* and what it *enforces*, discharge the
debt carried across three milestones, and finish the task-lifecycle discipline left unbuilt by v2.2
— so that every control the documentation claims exists actually fires, and every carried item
reaches a recorded disposition instead of a fourth carry.

**Thesis, from the v2.3 close:** a deny that is real for `Write`/`Edit` and absent for `bash` is not
a gate. ADR-0010 already states the principle — *"a layer that is only a data row is a claimed
control that does not exist — worse than a missing layer, because it reads as covered."* This
milestone applies that sentence to the harness itself.

## What the review changed

The reviewer's factual corrections are adopted in full. They are recorded here because several of
them correct claims this repo made *about itself*:

1. **The "ADR-0010 clause 3b overclaims" accusation is withdrawn.** Clause 3b enumerates the surface
   each of its three layers covers and scopes layer 1 explicitly to `PreToolUse(Write|Edit)`
   (`docs/adr/0010-...md:144-152`). It asserts no universal coverage. The defect is a **missing
   fourth layer for the bash surface**. Corrected in-repo by `455b366` and on PR #4.
2. **The deny domains are deliberately separate and must stay separate.** `ledger_guard` owns the
   review-ledger domain by design — ADR-0010 explains at `:152` and `:164` why it must NOT be folded
   into `CONSTITUTION_GLOBS`, and `commit_gate` does not enumerate plane paths at all (it gates
   contract drift, byte hygiene, golden parity). The draft's "one merged plane declaration" was
   wrong; the requirement is a **registry of distinct domains**, each audited against its own SSOT.
3. **"Resolve the effective write target of arbitrary bash" is not soundly implementable.**
   Interpreters, build tools, subprocesses, symlinks and generated scripts all defeat static
   command-text inference. A posture must be chosen *before* implementation, or the negative-control
   suite proves only the spellings its authors remembered. **This is the milestone's biggest risk.**
4. **RAT-5 is already discharged-with-caveats** (PR #4 merged; `STATE.md`). Not an open obligation.
5. **`DEF-05-02-1` was dropped from the draft** — three `commit_gate` tests leaking an ambient
   `GOLDEN_APPROVE_HUMAN` token (`STATE.md`, deferred at phase 05-02). It may already be repaired;
   the milestone must verify and close the record either way.
6. **The Phase-28/29 `human_needed` rows are acknowledged ratification rows, not implementation
   debt** — they must not be re-litigated as defects.

The reviewer recommended cutting Themes C *and* D and shipping five phases. The human kept C and cut
D. That is a deliberate, recorded deviation: it accepts a larger milestone than the reviewer advised,
against the reviewer's stated sizing risk.

## Theme A — Gate Integrity (`SEAL-*`)

- **SEAL-01** — A **deny-domain registry**: every path-deny domain in the harness
  (`contract_guard.CONSTITUTION_GLOBS`, `secret_scan.SECRET_PATH_GLOBS`,
  `ledger_guard.REVIEW_LEDGER_GLOBS`) is declared once with its **owner, its bypass semantics**
  (`GOLDEN_APPROVE_HUMAN` / `HARNESS_DEV_BYPASS` / none), **the tool surfaces it currently covers**,
  and **its runtime adapters**. Domains stay distinct — this is an inventory, not a merge. A
  `harness_lint` drift test fails when a hook's live constant disagrees with its declaration.
- **SEAL-02** — A **threat model and a chosen enforcement posture** for the uncovered bash surface,
  ratified as an ADR *before* any enforcement is written. The ADR must pick explicitly among:
  filesystem-level enforcement, a constrained command allowlist, protected command wrappers, or a
  documented residual boundary — and must state, in the ADR itself, what remains uncovered. An
  honest recorded residual is an acceptable outcome; an unbounded "spelling-independent" claim is not.
- **SEAL-03** — **Implement the posture SEAL-02 chose**, in both runtimes, with per-class deletion
  proofs and live negative controls, plus a superseding ADR entry recording the newly covered
  surface. Acceptance is stated against the *declared* posture, never against "all spellings".
- **SEAL-04** — **`secret_scan.py` reads its patterns from the contract** instead of hardcoding them
  (`tools/hooks/secret_scan.py:42`, carried since 26.2). The registry is already the SSOT for
  `adoption_scan` and `evidence/capture`; this hook is the last consumer that forked it.
- **SEAL-05** — **RAT-4 reaches a recorded disposition**, and the harness defines a **portable
  ratification record** — a checkable provenance artifact that does not depend on GitHub assigning a
  reviewer. Explicitly OUT of scope: repairing GitHub's trust model, branch protection, or reviewer
  eligibility. RAT-5 is marked closed-with-caveats, not reopened.

## Risks

1. **Sizing.** The reviewer advised five phases and this is eight, against explicit advice. v2.3
   planned six and shipped ten under adversarial-review pressure; Theme A is the most review-prone
   work in the repo because it is security-shaped. Expect insertions.
2. **Theme A is self-referential** — it changes the gates that gate the work doing the changing.
   Every step needs a live negative control or it self-blesses, and the `HARNESS_DEV_BYPASS` posture
   during the work needs stating up front.
3. **Phase 32 is the one that can expand without bound.** Its acceptance MUST be written against the
   posture ratified in 31, never against "all spellings". If phase 31 fails to pick a posture, stop
   rather than proceed.
4. **SEAL-05 may have no mechanism** — the honest outcome may be an ADR naming the limit.

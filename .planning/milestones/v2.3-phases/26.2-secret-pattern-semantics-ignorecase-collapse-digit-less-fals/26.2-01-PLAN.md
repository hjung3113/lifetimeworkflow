<objective>
Fix the two defects 26.1-REVIEW.md found in `secret_patterns[1]` of
`contracts/harness/task-control/gate-registry.json`: CR-01 (both consumers compile the registry
with `re.IGNORECASE`, which folds case inside `[A-Z]`/`[a-z]` character classes, so the documented
upper/lower diversity requirement is inert at runtime) and WR-01 (the unconditional digit
requirement drops digit-less secret shapes — passphrases, alphabetic tokens — that the pre-26.1
pattern used to catch, reaching `capture.py`'s evidence-redaction path where a miss writes a
secret in plaintext into a committed artifact). Per D-01 (human-decided this session), the fix
scopes the case lookaheads with Python 3.11's `(?-i:...)` inline-flag group (closing CR-01) and
relaxes the digit requirement to "length-floor + 2-of-3 charset classes" (any 2 of upper/lower/digit,
closing the mixed-case slice of WR-01's harm), re-verifying the SC-1 `ci.yml` false-positive proof
stays closed under the relaxation.

Purpose: Phase 27 broadens secret classification to arbitrary brownfield targets; shipping that on
top of a pattern whose documented semantics don't match its runtime behavior risks a real secret
reaching a committed evidence artifact (the more severe direction of risk vs. 26.1's over-exclusion
bug).
Output: data-based tests against both live consumer functions proving CR-01/WR-01's disposition, a
re-edited contract, a rebaselined hash manifest, a regenerated derived plane, and a human-ratified
constitution-plane commit — plus a recorded disposition for all four 26.1-REVIEW.md findings
(CR-01, WR-01, IN-01, IN-02).
</objective>

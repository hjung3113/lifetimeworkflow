## Overview

Close gap ③ of the owner's four-part purpose — *an LLM working in this repo understands cross-project
relationships better than it would in a generic repo* — under a binding no-growth constraint carried
from v2.5: never expand scope by adding verification gates, security layers or ceremony; the surface
may not grow without retiring at least as much.

The milestone held that line. Net surface change across four shipped phases: **+1 command**
(`/impact`, the one sanctioned addition), **±0 skills** (`harness-author` absorbed `skill-creator`,
8 → 8), **+0 gates, +0 CI jobs, +0 contracts, +0 `tools/` packages, +0 external dependencies**, and
nothing injected into SessionStart. `ci.yml`'s job set and `gate.needs` are byte-unchanged since the
milestone's first commit; only the existing `stale-derived` job's command list grew.

external precondition. Decided at kickoff, not inserted mid-flight. Decimal numbering was deliberately
not used: `50.1`/`50.2` are reserved for urgent insertions and would have misdescribed this.

residual blind spots.
